## 1. Customer-side subsidies: OP TAK, National Recovery Plan, vouchers, 2026 calls

### Takeaway
As of November 2026 no large national OP TAK call aimed at production robotization is open: the big "Technologie 4.0" call closed in early 2024 and the 2025-26 "Digitální podnik – Digitální technologie – výzva I" closed in spring 2026 and explicitly penalised production technology. The live route for small robot cells is the LEADER/CLLD call "Technologie pro MAS (CLLD) – výzva II" (50 % grant, max about CZK 1.5 million, rural areas, intake 1 Sept 2026 to 1 Sept 2027 via local action groups), plus Innovation Vouchers call IV for testing/development services. OP TAK is run by the Ministry of Industry and Trade (MPO) as managing authority, with Agentura pro podnikání a inovace (API) as the intermediate body.

### Inferences
- For an integrator's sales pitch in late 2026 and 2027, the only subsidy a typical small manufacturer can realistically attach to a single robot cell is Technologie pro MAS II (project up to about CZK 3 million, 50 %), and only if the plant is in a MAS territory outside larger towns. Cells priced just under CZK 3 million eligible cost fit this instrument well.
- Larger projects (CZK 5-50 million) depended on Technologie 4.0, which was heavily oversubscribed: 297 applications asking CZK 3.49 billion against a CZK 2.6 billion (raised) allocation implies roughly 75 % of requested volume could be funded at most, before formal rejections. That is an inferred ceiling, not a published success rate.
- The 2025-26 Digitální technologie call under-used its allocation (deadline was extended), consistent with its scoring penalty on production technology; it was not a robot-purchase instrument.
- OP TAK 2021-2027 is in its final phase; new large calls for production technology may not appear before the next programme period. Customers should not plan investment around a hoped-for call.

### Gaps
- The content of the 2026 call schedule XLSX (which calls are still planned for Q4 2026 and 2027) could not be opened.
- National Recovery Plan (Národní plán obnovy) component status: not searched successfully (budget exhausted). Unverified background: the NPO digital-transformation-of-enterprises component co-financed Digitální podnik/Technologie 4.0 calls and the RRF must be fully disbursed by end of August 2026, so no new NPO calls for robotization should be expected; verify on planobnovy.gov.cz.

- No official success-rate statistics per call were found.
- Regional (kraj-level) innovation/digital vouchers for customers were not found in-session (see section 4 gaps).

## 2. Tax incentives: depreciation, R&D deduction, investment incentives

### Takeaway
The R&D tax deduction became more generous from 1 January 2026 (150 % of eligible R&D costs up to CZK 50 million per group, 100 % above), which matters to an integrator that develops its own cells or software. Investment incentives were under revision in 2025 with a proposal to lower thresholds for strategic technology centres; their 2026 status is unconfirmed. Nothing was confirmed in-session on extraordinary depreciation for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 Jan 2026 the R&D deduction is 150 % of R&D expenses up to a CZK 50 million limit; expenses above the limit are deductible at 100 %; the former 110 % incremental deduction was abolished; unused deduction can be carried forward for five tax periods instead of three. — [Ministry of Finance](https://mf.gov.cz/cs/ministerstvo/media/tiskove-zpravy/2026/prehledne-ktere-zmeny-pripravilo-ministerstvo-fina-62343)
- The CZK 50 million limit applies to the whole "odpočtový celek" (group of related entities), not per company. — [Grantex](https://grantex.cz/blog/nove-vymezeni-oblasti-vyzkumu-a-vyvoje/); [Daňovky](https://danovky.cz/cs/odpocet-na-vyzkum-a-vyvoj-od-roku-2026-co-se-zmeni-a-co-zustava)
- The limit is pro-rated to the length of the tax period. — [PKF Apogeo](https://www.pkfapogeo.cz/en/blog/7824/odpocet-vydaju-na-vyzkum-a-vyvoj-zmeny-od-112026)
- Case law of the Supreme Administrative Court sets stricter evidence requirements for the R&D deduction than the statute's formal wording. — [dReport](https://www.dreport.cz/blog/novela-odpoctu-na-vyzkum-a-vyvoj-v-kontextu-aktualni-judikatury/)
- Investment incentives (Act 72/2000 Sb.): a 2025 draft amendment to the implementing government regulation would cut the requirement for strategic technology centres from CZK 200 million to CZK 50 million of investment and from 70 to 20 new jobs, add a 150 % "added effect" test (profit + wages + contributions), and give bonuses for districts with higher unemployment. — [EY tax alert, July 2025](https://www.ey.com/cs_cz/technical/tax/tax-alerts/2025/07/navrh-novely-narizeni-vlady-o-provedeni-zakona-o-investicnich-pobidkach)
- The explanatory report states the technology-centre aid regime is notified until end of 2026; MPO registered 36 incentive applications by May 2025, 9 of them strategic. — [Explanatory report via Zákony pro lidi](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/media2/file/2507/File76847.pdf?attachment-filename=8095418-2025-07-01-duvodova-zprava-8098362.pdf)
- The 2019 amendment (in force 6 Sept 2019) halved the eligibility conditions for SMEs. — [MPO](https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/podnikani/dotace-a-podpora-podnikani/investicni-pobidky-a-prumyslove-zony/investicni-pobidky/novela-zakona-c--72-2000-sb---o-investicnich-pobidkach----223105/)

### Inferences
- Investment incentives are designed for sizeable investments and are unlikely to matter for a typical single-cell purchase by a small manufacturer or for a startup integrator; the R&D deduction is the realistic tax tool for an integrator once it is profitable.
- Because the R&D deduction needs a written project document before work starts and is frequently challenged, a small firm should budget for adviser support.

### Gaps
- Extraordinary/accelerated depreciation in 2026: no source obtained (search budget exhausted). Unverified background: the temporary "mimořádné odpisy" under section 30a of the Income Tax Act (12 months for group 1, 24 months for group 2) applied to assets acquired 2020-2023 and I have no evidence it was extended to 2026; most machinery and robots sit in depreciation group 2 (5 years) with a choice of straight-line or accelerated (zrychlené) method. Verify with the current Income Tax Act.
- Whether the 2025 investment-incentive regulation amendment was adopted, and its 2026 text, is unconfirmed.

## 3. Financing instruments: NRB loans and guarantees, leasing, robot-as-a-service

### Takeaway
The National Development Bank (NRB) reopened zero-interest "Expanze – úvěry OPTAK" investment loans on 2 February 2026 (CZK 1-100 million, up to 50 % of eligible costs, up to 15 years, outside Prague) but suspended intake from 10 August 2026 because the call's funds were fully subscribed. A new portfolio guarantee, "Garant InvestEU" (up to 70 % guarantee, arranged directly through the client's commercial bank), is planned to reach end-borrowers in Q4 2026. Robot rental and RaaS exist in Czechia but are a niche; published prices are sparse and old.

### Cited Findings
- NRB began taking applications for zero-interest Expanze loans (call II) on 2 Feb 2026, for sole traders and SMEs; total allocation CZK 5.13 billion; target about a thousand SME investment projects outside Prague. — [NRB](https://www.nrb.cz/nrb-zacina-prijimat-zadosti-o-bezurocne-uvery-expanze/); [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/nrb-zacina-prijimat-zadosti-o-bezurocne-uvery-expanze-podpori-investice-malych-firem/)
- Terms: loan CZK 1-100 million at 0 % interest; NRB can cover up to 50 % of eligible expenses; maturity up to 15 years; repayment deferral up to 3 years; a partner bank or leasing company must co-finance at least 20 % of project costs. — [Grantuj](https://www.grantuj.cz/l/expanze-uvery-optak-vyzva-ii-bezurocne-financovani-investic-pro-male-a-stredni-podniky/); [MPO press release](https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/rozcestnik/pro-media/tiskove-zpravy/narodni-rozvojova-banka-spousti-klicovy-uverovy-financni-nastroj-expanze-uvery-optak-pro-male-a-stredni-podniky--291221/)
- One source gives the nominal application window as 2 Feb 2026 to 30 June 2029. — [Grantuj](https://www.grantuj.cz/l/expanze-uvery-optak-vyzva-ii-bezurocne-financovani-investic-pro-male-a-stredni-podniky/)
- NRB suspended intake of Expanze loan applications from 10 August 2026 because the volume of applications reached the funds set aside for the current call. — [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/rozvojova-banka-pozastavila-prijem-zadosti-o-zvyhodnene-uvery-expanze/)
- Family-business track opened 15 June 2026: interest-free investment loan up to CZK 120 million, deferral up to 4 years, last 10 % of the loan forgiven. — [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/program-expanze-mpo-a-nrb-podpori-rodinne-podniky-specialnimi-bezurocnymi-uvery/); [Kurzy.cz](https://zpravy.kurzy.cz/863167-nrb-zacina-prijimat-zadosti-rodinnych-podniku-o-zvyhodnene-uvery-v-programu-expanze/)
- Garant InvestEU: NRB pilot portfolio-guarantee programme for SMEs and small mid-caps; guarantee up to 70 % of loan principal; guarantee term 3 years for working-capital and 10 years for investment loans; fee 0.2 % p.a. of outstanding guarantees; the firm deals only with its lending bank. — [NRB product page](https://www.nrb.cz/produkt/zaruka-invest-eu/); [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/mensim-podnikum-pomuze-novy-financni-nastroj-spousti-se-garant-investeu/)
- The open call for intermediary banks ran to 30 July 2026 and launch for end applicants is planned for Q4 2026. — [NRB](https://www.nrb.cz/vysledky-otevrene-vyzvy-u-programu-garant-investeu-potvrzuji-velky-zajem-o-zaruky-mezi-bankami/)
- An EC counter-guarantee of EUR 80 million (about CZK 2 billion) from InvestEU increases the capacity of the Záruka 2024-2030 programme; the "Národní záruka" programme passed CZK 2 billion, with guaranteed loans from CZK 500 thousand to CZK 20 million. — [MPO](https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/rozcestnik/pro-media/tiskove-zpravy/program-narodni-zaruka-prekonal-milnik-2-mld--kc--292624/); [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/novy-zarucni-program-nrb-pritahuje-firmy-po-ctvrt-roce-se-priblizil-miliarde/)
- Guaranteed-loan size is inconsistent across sources: NRB's product page extract shows CZK 40-60 million for Garant InvestEU, press shows up to CZK 20 million for Národní záruka, and a partner bank's page shows CZK 500 thousand to CZK 40 million, up to 7 years, guarantee up to 80 %. — [NRB](https://www.nrb.cz/produkt/zaruka-invest-eu/); [Trinity Bank](https://trinitybank.cz/file/786)
- Guarantees can now be arranged directly at the client's bank instead of with NRB. — [e15](https://www.e15.cz/finexpert/banky-a-ucty/konec-obihani-uradu-statni-zaruku-za-uver-ted-vyridite-primo-ve-sve-bance-1434528)
- Leasing companies can be the co-financing partner for NRB-supported investment (e.g. KB's SGEF). — [KB SGEF](https://www.kb.cz/cs/sgef/spolupracujeme/financovani-s-podporou-nrb)
- Robot rental: SMAR s.r.o. (ABB partner) advertises industrial-robot rental from CZK 29,500 per month on a 60-month contract including installation, spare parts and service (page date unclear). — [SMAR](https://erp.smar.cz/en_US/page/operativni-leasing)
- Older data points: AMTECH offered cobot rental for 1-4 years, about CZK 65,000 per month for its largest cobot on a one-year term (article about eight years old); Rob4Job's "Robotolling" was designed for three-year terms and two-shift use (2019). — [ITpoint/AMTECH](https://www.itpoint.cz/amtech/?i=pronajem-robotu-vyroba-logistika-12842); [Robotic Journal 2019](https://www.roboticjournal.cz/clanky/2019-cislo-3-nemate-lidi-pujcte-si-roboty)
- Czech commentary describes RaaS as subscription/opex including installation and programming, and one recent article says Czech firms still do not offer RaaS to a significant extent; other providers (e.g. Robotinvest) advertise temporary assignment, rental and sale of robots. — [Svět robotů](https://www.svet-robotu.cz/blogs/novinky/automatizace-bez-vstupni-investice-jak-z-0-kc-udelat-stabilni-zisk-raas); [Innovation Anywhere](https://innovationanywhere.cz/robot-as-a-service/); [Robotinvest](https://robotinvest.cz/)

### Inferences
- With Expanze suspended since August 2026, a customer buying in late 2026 mostly has commercial bank loans/leasing, optionally with an NRB guarantee; a reopening of Expanze cannot be assumed.
- The mandatory 20 % co-financing by a partner bank or leasing company means Expanze, when open, combines naturally with equipment leasing of the robot cell.
- A thin RaaS market suggests an opening for an integrator to offer rental through a leasing partner, but residual-value and re-deployment risk would sit with the integrator.

### Gaps
- Whether and when a further Expanze call opens after the August 2026 suspension is unknown.
- No current (2026) Czech price list for cobot/robot operating leases was found.
- Exact current loan-size limits for NRB guarantees are inconsistent across sources and need checking on nrb.cz.

## 4. Supplier-side support for a startup integrator

### Takeaway

## 5. Legal form and setup: s.r.o., trade licences, electrical qualifications

### Takeaway
An s.r.o. can be founded with CZK 1 capital in roughly one to three weeks for about CZK 5,500-11,000 all-in, but the notarial deed is still required. For automation work the company needs craft trades (řemeslné živnosti) "Výroba, instalace, opravy elektrických strojů a přístrojů, elektronických a telekomunikačních zařízení" and, for work on reserved electrical equipment, "Montáž, opravy, revize a zkoušky elektrických zařízení", each backed by a qualified responsible representative (odpovědný zástupce); separately it needs a TIČR authorisation under Act 250/2021 Sb., and each person doing electrical work needs personal competence under NV 194/2022 Sb. (sections 6 to 8).

### Inferences
- Three layers must be kept apart: (1) the trade licence with a responsible representative, (2) the company-level TIČR authorisation for work on reserved electrical equipment, and (3) each worker's personal NV 194/2022 competence. A founder who is a section 7 "vedoucí elektrotechnik" with relevant education can typically cover layer 1 as responsible representative and anchor layers 2 and 3; otherwise the firm must employ or contract such a person.
- Because section 7 needs two to four years of documented practice, a founding team without an experienced electrical engineer cannot self-qualify quickly; this is a real gating item for a new integrator that builds and wires its own control cabinets.
- Whether building a machine's own electrical equipment (control cabinet, machine wiring to EN 60204-1) in the workshop counts as work on "reserved electrical equipment" needing the TIČR authorisation, as opposed to connecting the machine to the plant installation on site, is not answered by the sources seen; it should be confirmed with TIČR.

### Gaps
- The task brief describes the electrical trade as a "regulated/bound" (vázaná) trade; sources found place both electrical trades in Annex 1 (craft trades). I could not open the statute text to confirm the current annex wording for 2026.
- Trades for the mechanical side were not sourced. Unverified background: "Zámečnictví, nástrojářství" and "Obráběčství" are craft trades, while "Výroba strojů a zařízení" is a field of the single free trade (volná živnost); software/PLC programming and engineering design fall under free-trade fields. - TIČR authorisation procedure, fee and processing time were not found.

## 6. Tax and employment basics

### Takeaway
VAT registration becomes mandatory after turnover exceeds CZK 2,000,000 in a calendar year (from 1 January of the next year) or immediately after exceeding CZK 2,536,500. Supplying a machine with installation to a customer in another EU state is taxed where it is installed, usually by reverse charge to a VAT-registered customer. Using sole-trader contractors in an employee-like way risks fines of CZK 50,000 to 10 million. PLC programmers earn roughly CZK 45-65 thousand gross per month on job-portal averages.

### Cited Findings

VAT
- Two thresholds apply under the amended VAT Act: exceeding CZK 2,000,000 turnover in a calendar year makes the business a payer from 1 January of the following year; exceeding CZK 2,536,500 (about EUR 100,000) makes it a payer from the day after the limit is passed. — [Shoptet blog](https://blog.shoptet.cz/registrace-dph-zmeny/); [Dokvero](https://dokvero.cz/navody/limit-obratu-dph-2026)
- The registration application is due within 10 working days of crossing a threshold; voluntary registration is also possible. — [Fakturoid](https://www.fakturoid.cz/almanach/dane/prechod-na-platce-dph); [ucto.cz](https://ucto.cz/dph/registrace-k-dph)
- One guide still describes the test as 12 consecutive months rather than the calendar year (sources disagree; most say calendar year). — [ainvoice](https://ainvoice.cz/blog/kdy-se-stat-platcem-dph/)
- For a supply of goods with installation or assembly, the place of supply is where the goods are installed or assembled (section 7 of the VAT Act; older articles cite subsection 6). If installed in another member state, Czech VAT does not apply. — [Money.cz](https://money.cz/novinky-a-tipy/dane/dodani-zbozi-s-montazi-ci-instalaci-z-pohledu-dph/); [Delfy](http://delfy.cz/ucetni-podpora/dph-u-zbozi/misto-plneni-pri-dodani-zbozi/)
- Such a supply is not an exempt intra-Community supply of goods and is not reported in the recapitulative statement (souhrnné hlášení); if the customer is VAT-registered in the destination state the tax is typically accounted for by the customer under reverse charge, and if not, the Czech supplier must register there (example in source concerned Slovakia). — [Money.cz](https://money.cz/novinky-a-tipy/dane/dodani-zbozi-s-montazi-ci-instalaci-z-pohledu-dph/); [e-Pravo](https://www.epravo.cz/top/clanky/problematika-dodani-zbozi-do-jineho-clenskeho-statu-z-pohledu-dph-108290.html)
- The Financial Administration publishes guidance for VAT payers on selected provisions for cross-border supplies. — [Finanční správa](https://financnisprava.gov.cz/cs/mezinarodni-spoluprace/mezinarodni-spoluprace-a-dph/informace-pro-platce-dph-k-vybranym-ustanovenim-zakona)

Contractors versus employees ("švarcsystém")
- The relationship is judged on how the work is really performed, not on the contract name; working for one client alone does not automatically make it švarcsystém; risk markers include fixed working hours, company instructions and regular invoicing of the same amount; converting a former employee to a contractor with the same job is especially risky. — [dokladbot](https://dokladbot.cz/blog/svarcsystem-znaky-sankce-jak-se-vyhnout); [Průvodce podnikáním](https://www.pruvodcepodnikanim.cz/clanek/legalni-spoluprace-s-osvc/)
- Penalties: the firm faces a fine of CZK 50,000 to CZK 10,000,000, and since 2025 also a ban on activity for up to 2 years and publication of the decision; the contractor faces up to CZK 100,000. — [AK Šmarda](https://www.aksmarda.cz/rady-a-tipy/kontrola-svarcsystemu-pokuty-prubeh-obrana/); [dokladbot](https://dokladbot.cz/blog/svarcsystem-znaky-sankce-jak-se-vyhnout)
- Courts have been tightening their view of švarcsystém, with risk of back-assessed tax and contributions. — [Měšec.cz](https://www.mesec.cz/clanky/soudy-zprisnuji-pohled-na-svarcsystem-za-co-uz-by-mohla-hrozit-pokuta-nebo-domerky/)
- A change to the definition of dependent work was in the legislative process after the Rohlík case; one recent source says a new definition applies from 1 December 2026 with four cumulative conditions the authority must prove; an older source says the definition narrows to two features (subordination and work in the employer's name); a proposal would abolish the fine for the worker while keeping fines up to CZK 10 million for firms. Sources disagree and the final text is unconfirmed. — [dokladbot](https://dokladbot.cz/blog/svarcsystem-znaky-sankce-jak-se-vyhnout); [AK Fráňa](https://www.akfrana.cz/post/svarcsystem---nova-definice-zavisle-prace-v-legislativnim-procesu); [e15](https://www.e15.cz/finexpert/prace-a-mzda/kauza-rohlik-jako-varovani-pravidla-pro-svarcsystem-se-maji-zmenit-co-ceka-praci-na-ico-1436348)

### Inferences
- A B2B integrator will normally register for VAT voluntarily at formation to recover input VAT on robots and components, so the thresholds matter little in practice.
- For a German or Austrian turnkey project the invoice is usually issued without Czech VAT with reverse charge at the customer, but the supplier must confirm the destination state's rule (e.g. German section 13b UStG) and any foreign registration duty before invoicing; pure services (programming, commissioning) to an EU business follow the general B2B rule and should be checked separately.
- Budgeting employer cost for an experienced PLC programmer: gross CZK 55-80 thousand per month is a defensible planning range from these portals, with Prague and senior commissioning engineers at the top.

## 7. Insurance

### Takeaway
Czech insurers sell general liability with product-liability and "pure financial loss" extensions as part of business packages (e.g. ČPP Komplex with limits up to CZK 100 million); market commentary suggests CZK 5-20 million limits for small companies, but no published premium for a machine builder or integrator was found. Quotes must come from a broker.

### Cited Findings
- ČPP's "Komplex" business package advertises liability limits up to CZK 100 million. — [ČPP](https://www.cpp.cz/pojisteni-podnikatelu/pojisteni-podnikatelu-komplex)
- ČSOB Pojišťovna liability terms: for pure financial loss the insurer pays up to the agreed limit but at most CZK 30 million, whichever is lower. — [ČSOB VPP](https://www.csob.cz/documents/10710/1599957/vpp-pojisteni-odpovednosti.pdf)
- Generali Česká terms: indemnity is paid up to the agreed limit, with special sub-limits possible for product-defect risk. — [Generali Česká](https://www.generaliceska.cz/documents/20183/64633/pojisteni_odpovednosti_podnikani.pdf/23fff7be-745a-413d-b112-78e55c90f586)
- Product-liability supplementary terms treat the cover as indemnity insurance with manufacturer-specific deductibles/conditions. — [ČPP DPP product liability](https://www.dumpojistoven.cz/download/pojistne-podminky/ceska-podnikatelska-pojistovna/2011/2011-03-01-1652344710-doplnkove-pojistne-podminky-pro-pojisteni-odpovednosti-za-skodu-zpusobenou-vadn%C3%BDm-v%C3%BDrobkem-dppvv-p-1.11-1.3.2011.pdf)
- Indicative limits: about CZK 5-10 million is described as standard for an s.r.o. with a few employees; another commentary recommends at least CZK 10-20 million. — [Fakturaonline](https://www.fakturaonline.cz/podnikateluv-pruvodce/finance-a-ucetnictvi/zalohy-pojisteni-platby/pojisteni-odpovednosti-pro-osvc-kdy-ho-potrebujete-a-kolik-stoji); [Total Brokers](https://totalbrokers.cz/cs/magazin/pojisteni-odpovednosti-pro-firmy-online/)
- Indicative prices for sole traders: general liability mostly CZK 1,500-5,000 per year, professional liability CZK 3,000-10,000 per year; higher-risk trades pay more. — [Fakturaonline](https://www.fakturaonline.cz/podnikateluv-pruvodce/finance-a-ucetnictvi/zalohy-pojisteni-platby/pojisteni-odpovednosti-pro-osvc-kdy-ho-potrebujete-a-kolik-stoji)
- Liability cover can include damage caused by a defective product and by defective work after handover; specialist brokers offer professional-liability products. — [Nablbost.cz](https://www.nablbost.cz/pojisteni-profesni-odpovednosti)

### Inferences
- An integrator needs three things checked in the policy wording: defective product and defective work after handover, pure financial loss (customer's production downtime) with its sub-limit, and territorial scope covering Germany/Austria if it installs there.
- Sole-trader premiums of a few thousand crowns are not a guide for a machine builder; expect a materially higher, turnover-rated premium.

### Gaps
- No Czech source gave premiums or typical limits specifically for machine builders or system integrators.
- No information found on recall-cost cover, contractor's all-risk/assembly (montážní) insurance pricing, or customer-required limits in Czech automotive supply contracts.

## 8. Czech implementation of EU machinery safety rules

### Takeaway
Until 19 January 2027 machinery is governed by NV 176/2008 Sb. (Czech transposition of Directive 2006/42/EC); from 20 January 2027 Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies directly and Act 236/2024 Sb. repeals NV 176/2008 on that date. Instructions and the declaration of conformity must be in Czech for machines supplied in Czechia. An integrator delivering cells in early 2027 must plan which regime applies to each machine by its placing-on-the-market date.

### Cited Findings
- Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 was published 29 June 2023, becomes fully binding from 20 January 2027, replaces Directive 2006/42/EC, and is directly applicable without national transposition. — [ABB Czech FAQ](https://new.abb.com/low-voltage/cs/nizke-napeti/produkty/bezpecnostni-systemy/narizeni-eu); [EUR-Lex (Czech text)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj/ces)
- Act 236/2024 Sb. repeals NV 176/2008 Sb. with effect from 20 January 2027 and adapts Czech product legislation to the EU regulation; machinery placed on the market before 20 January 2027 under NV 176/2008 may still be supplied afterwards. — [e-Pravo, Act 236/2024 Sb.](https://www.epravo.cz/top/zakony/sbirka-zakonu/sb/2024/236); [Právní prostor](https://pravniprostor.cz/zmeny-v-legislative/vyslo-ve-sbirce-zakonu/novela-zakona-o-posuzovani-shody-stanovenych-vyrobku-pri-jejich-dodavani-na-trh2)
- The regulation applies only to machines placed on the market from 20 January 2027; existing machines meeting the directive may continue to be used. — [Práce a mzda](https://www.praceamzda.cz/clanky/18253/nove-narizeni-evropskeho-parlamentu-o-strojnich-zarizenich)
- ÚNMZ (Czech Office for Standards, Metrology and Testing) publishes information for manufacturers on the Machinery Regulation. — [ÚNMZ](https://unmz.gov.cz/en/narizeni-o-strojnich-zarizenich-informace-pro-vyrobce/)
- Technical requirements for machinery are currently set by NV 176/2008 Sb.; an overview of Czech machinery legislation is maintained by NLFnorm. — [CIVOP](https://www.civop.cz/prohlaseni-o-shode/); [NLFnorm](https://nlfnorm.cz/en/informacni-portal/182/pravni-predpisy-cr-pro-strojni-zarizeni)
- The EC declaration of conformity must be in Czech and include notified-body details where one was involved; the manufacturer keeps the original at least 10 years from the last day of manufacture. — [BOZPprofi](https://www.bozpprofi.cz/33/pruvodni-dokumentace-strojnich-zarizeni-uniqueidmRRWSbk196FNf8-jVUh4Egny2sdjJ1sRjWiM1G86sLqw9PBWuC7BmQ/)
- Instructions must be in Czech and address reasonably foreseeable misuse; either the original instructions or a translation marked as a translation, accompanied by the original, must be supplied in the official language of the country of use. — [BOZPprofi](https://www.bozpprofi.cz/33/pruvodni-dokumentace-strojnich-zarizeni-uniqueidmRRWSbk196FNf8-jVUh4Egny2sdjJ1sRjWiM1G86sLqw9PBWuC7BmQ/); [QMprofi](https://www.qmprofi.cz/33/podklady-k-sestaveni-navodu-k-pouzivani-strojniho-zarizeni-uniqueidmRRWSbk196FNf8-jVUh4EvD6cPpZTsNIGtAs4UC1fR4/)
- For some machinery a notified body takes part in conformity assessment and issues a certificate, while the declaration is always issued by the manufacturer. — [euprohlasenioshode.cz](https://euprohlasenioshode.cz/); [Komora CERT](https://www.komoracert.cz/posuzovani-a-prohlasovani-shody-vyrobku/posouzeni-vyrobcem/posouzeni-shody-strojni-zarizeni/)
- TÜV SÜD announced in October 2024 that it was the first notified body in the world designated for the new Machinery Regulation (Czech-language press release). — [TÜV SÜD CZ](https://www.tuvsud.com/cs-cz/tisk-a-media/2024/rijen/tuv-sud-je-prvni-notifikovana-osoba-na-svete-pro-nove-narizeni-o-strojnich-zarizenich)
- Vendor material (Siemens CZ) summarises changes including digital operating instructions under the new regulation. — [Siemens CZ PDF](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:f724d4e7-fd4f-4e6b-8d4b-ac302ef54e21/zmeny-v-novem-strojnim-narizeni-2023-1230.pdf)

### Inferences
- An integrator that combines a robot (partly completed machinery) with tooling, guarding and controls is the manufacturer of the resulting cell and must issue the Czech-language declaration and instructions itself; this holds under both regimes.
- For projects contracted in late 2026 but handed over after 20 January 2027, contracts should state which regime the cell is assessed under; the safest course is to design to the regulation's requirements now.

### Gaps
- Market surveillance by Česká obchodní inspekce (ČOI) for machinery: not sourced (search budget exhausted). Unverified background: ČOI is the market surveillance authority for machinery placed on the market, while workplace use is inspected by the labour inspectorate (SÚIP/OIP).

Unverified background: Strojírenský zkušební ústav (SZÚ) Brno, TÜV SÜD Czech and others hold or held machinery notifications; TZÚS Praha is mainly a construction-products body. ## 9. Cross-border work in Germany and Austria (summary)

### Takeaway
A Czech company sending its own employees to install or commission machines needs an A1 certificate from the Czech Social Security Administration for each worker, must respect host-country minimum pay, and must check notification duties: Germany's online Meldeportal-Mindestlohn applies only to listed sectors, while Austria requires an online posting notification and applies collective-agreement wages. No work permits are needed for Czech citizens.

### Cited Findings
- The Czech Labour Office/MPO guide "Jak správně přeshraničně poskytovat služby a vysílat pracovníky" was updated in March 2026. — [Úřad práce PDF](https://up.gov.cz/cms/documents/f5d30a1b-344e-e6ef-833e-f283683bdb35/Poskytovani_preshranicnich_sluzeb_vysilani_pracovniku.pdf)
- A1 certifies that the worker stays in the Czech social-security system; it is issued by the Czech (or district) Social Security Administration; posted workers should carry it; it need not be translated; a standard posting can be certified for up to 24 months. — [Úřad práce PDF](https://up.gov.cz/cms/documents/f5d30a1b-344e-e6ef-833e-f283683bdb35/Poskytovani_preshranicnich_sluzeb_vysilani_pracovniku.pdf); [MPO](https://mpo.gov.cz/dokument746.html)
- Germany: notification is made online via the Meldeportal-Mindestlohn to customs (Zoll), in advance, only for the listed sectors; paper or fax is no longer accepted; a one-off registration creates the account; changes must be reported through the portal. — [HWK Niederbayern-Oberpfalz guide, Jan 2026](https://www.hwkno.de/downloads/cz-vykonavani-remeslnickych-sluzeb-v-nemecku-76,18084.pdf)
- Germany: where a sector has a generally binding collectively agreed minimum wage higher than the statutory minimum, it takes precedence. — [HWK Niederbayern-Oberpfalz guide, Jan 2026](https://www.hwkno.de/downloads/cz-vykonavani-remeslnickych-sluzeb-v-nemecku-76,18084.pdf)
- Austria: an online notification form exists, also in Czech; minimum wages are set by generally binding collective agreements; construction work additionally requires contributions to the holiday fund. — [Czech MFA guide for Austria (labelled updated Aug 2026)](https://mzv.gov.cz/file/4668948/Legislativni_nalezitosti_vysilani_pracovniku_a_pusobeni_ceskych_OSVC_a_firem_v_Rakousku_verze_listopad_2022.pdf)
- Transitional restrictions have ended, so work permits can no longer be required of Czech citizens in Germany or Austria. — [MPO](https://mpo.gov.cz/dokument86470.html)

### Inferences
- For Germany the HWK guide exists because many technical activities count as regulated crafts (Handwerk); a Czech integrator doing electrical installation in Germany should check whether a notification of cross-border service provision to the competent Handwerkskammer is needed in addition to A1 and wage rules.
- Short commissioning trips are low-friction if A1s are obtained in advance; the administrative load rises for long on-site installations and for any work classed as construction.

### Gaps
- Austria's ZKO 3 notification, the LSD-BG wage-dumping act, required on-site documents and any assembly exemption ("Montageprivileg") for supplied machinery were not sourced (search budget exhausted). Unverified background: Austria requires a ZKO 3 posting notification before work starts and wage documents on site, with a limited exemption for assembly/commissioning of delivered plant by the supplier's staff for short periods.
- Whether machine installation/commissioning falls within Germany's notifiable sectors, and the German initial-assembly exemption, were not confirmed.
- Host-country VAT registration and permanent-establishment thresholds for long installation projects were not researched.
- Recognition of Czech electrical qualifications (NV 194/2022) in Germany/Austria was not researched.
